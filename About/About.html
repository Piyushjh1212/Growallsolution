The problem we set out to solve

Kuch saal pehle, humne ek pattern notice kiya. Students degrees toh le rahe the, tutorials toh dekh rahe the, par jab baat actual jobs ki ya khud ka kuch build karne ki aati thi, toh confidence missing tha.

Market ki requirement kuch aur thi, aur colleges/courses mein sikhaya kuch aur ja raha tha. "Tutorial Hell" mein phanse hue students the, jinke paas theory ki knowledge thi, par hands-on experience zero.

Wahin se shuruat hui Grow All Coaching ki.

"Hamara goal ek simple platform banana nahi tha. Hamara goal ek aisi community banana tha jahan seekhna ek process nahi, ek experience ban jaye."

How we fix it

Humne pure learning model ko flip kar diya. Hamare yahan "Ratta maar" concept exist hi nahi karta.

Hum vishwas rakhte hain Project-Based Learning mein. Aap tab tak effectively nahi seekh sakte, jab tak aap apne haath gande karke code/design ya build na karein. Isliye, hamara har course, har module is tarah design kiya gaya hai ki aap day one se output create kar rahe hon.

Boring PPTs ki jagah live coding, real-life case studies, aur industry-vetted mentors. Hum aapko sirf concepts nahi sikhate, hum aapko wo mindset dete hain jo ek successful professional ka hota hai.
